How we score

GA: 0–100 pre-game strength from ranked history — nothing from the game itself counts.

GA components

ComponentPtsHow
Form27last-5 W/L ×22, streak ±5, tilt −3, rust −2, AFK −2
Performance25avg KDA (capped 10) ×5
Champion comfort20games on champ ×4 + mastery, OTP-denied −5
Role heat153 + 12 × role share of last 5
Rank vs lobby10tier vs lobby avg, smurf → 10
Season winrate5WR tiers, needs 20+ season games

Lane verdicts

adjusted GA = GA − 5 autofill − 5 first-time − 8 countered
EVEN ≤8 · favored 9–18 · heavy >18

NOT FAIR if any of

  • Team GA gap ≥12 (incl. duo bonus, cap 8)
  • Tier spread inside a team ≥2.5
  • Risk asymmetry (autofill/first-time) ≥8
  • 2+ lanes heavily favored one side
  • Blowout lane ≥30 GA
  • Net lane advantage ≥35 GA
  • 2+ first-time champs on your team
Shown as VS YOU / FOR YOU relative to the profile analyzed.
