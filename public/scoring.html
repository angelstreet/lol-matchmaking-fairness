How we score

GA: 0–100 pre-game strength from ranked history — nothing from the game itself counts.

Lane verdicts

adjusted GA = GA − 5 autofill − counter (role-weighted) + 3 duo'd (+9/+12/+15 if the duo includes a jungler, adaptive — see below) ± 2/division head-to-head rank gap (capped ±8) ± bot-lane duo synergy (BOTTOM/UTILITY only, capped ±4 — see below)
EVEN ≤5 · favored 6–18 · heavy >18 — except when exactly one laner is autofilled: that lane never reads EVEN (the non-autofilled side gets at least +1) even if the adjusted numbers land inside the band, since off-role risk is an inherent edge the raw gap doesn't fully capture.
Counter penalty is role-weighted, not flat: −8 top/mid (solo lanes, no one to bail them out), −4 jungle, −3 bot/supp (a partner — jungler ganks, lane duo — can offset a bad matchup). lib/counters.mjs itself stays a clean binary "known counter or not" matrix; the GA cost is scaled at the lane the countered player is actually in.
The duo bonus applies to whichever lane each half of a duo pair is playing — separate from (and on top of) the team-level duo bonus below. A same-lane duo gets a flat +3. A jungle-inclusive duo's bonus is ADAPTIVE instead — it scales with how far behind the duo'd laner's raw GA deficit is vs their direct lane opponent (deficit <10 → +9, 10–19 → +12, ≥20 → +15; a duo'd laner who's ahead still gets the baseline +9, never a penalty). Real cases drove this: a duo'd laner going 11/4/12 through a ~34-point GA deficit, and another MVP-ing at 1/8/27 — the box score alone read as a stomp, but the jungler had been propping the lane up the whole game. A jungler can gank/path on call for their duo partner in a way a same-lane duo can't replicate for a third teammate — and the worse that partner's lane is going, the harder a jungler actually camps it.
Rank is now shown directly on each player (a compact tag next to GA — "E4 · 51%", "M" alone for Master+ with no games yet — hover for full rank + LP + W-L) — a real gap could previously be completely invisible in the lane table. The lane math itself also weighs the direct HEAD-TO-HEAD rank gap between the two lane opponents specifically: 2 GA per division of gap (tier×4 + division, Master+ collapsed to tier base), capped at ±8 total, favoring the higher-ranked side. This is separate from and additional to the rank-vs-lobby-average signal already baked into each player's GA — that one measures a player against the whole lobby's tier; this measures the direct matchup.
Every champion name in the matchup table (dashed underline, hover) shows its current meta — win/pick/ban rate — from a static per-patch snapshot (lib/champstats.mjs, community-editable and refreshed per patch, same spirit as the counter matrix); info only, no scoring impact. A champion missing from the snapshot just shows as plain text, no hover.
Bot-lane (ADC+SUPPORT) duo synergy — a duo +2.4%/duo −1.8% chip on the BOTTOM and UTILITY rows, from lib/duosynergy.mjs's per-patch snapshot (op.gg's per-champion "synergies with support" data). The number is the pair's own winrate minus the MEAN of both champs' individual solo winrates (lib/champstats.mjs) — so it isolates "does this specific pairing work," not just "are both champs individually strong." Modest by design: each side's value is capped at ±4 GA and only applied to that side's own BOTTOM/UTILITY lane deltas; a missing pairing (most ADC+SUPPORT combos are — coverage is each ADC's top-10 partners by play count, not exhaustive) means no chip and no adjustment, never a guessed number. TEAM footer shows both sides' values side by side when both have data.
Smurf-flagged players skip the autofill term entirely (0, not just reduced) — the countered term still applies.

NOT FAIR triggers (below), but net decides

  • Team GA gap ≥12 (incl. duo bonus, cap 24)
  • Tier spread inside a team ≥2.5
  • Risk asymmetry (autofill) ≥8
  • 2+ lanes heavily favored one side
  • Blowout lane ≥30 GA
  • Net lane advantage ≥35 GA
Team GA = 65% team avg + 35% avg of top 2 GAs (not a flat average).
Duo bonus per pair: 8 with a winning joint record together, 6 mixed, 4 losing, +0 with no shared prior game to judge by — plus a hot-duo streak bonus on top: +3 if BOTH partners are individually on a 3+ win streak right now, +5 if both are on a 5+ win streak (one partner streaking alone doesn't count). A team with 2+ duos gets its summed bonus amplified ×1.35 (v4.6, up from ×1.25 — 4 players coordinating vs 2), capped at 24.
net = 0.5 × (signed lane-delta sum, per-lane contribution capped at ±15) + team GA gap + 1.5 × duo bonus difference + (enemy autofill weight − your autofill weight) + (your bot synergy − enemy bot synergy, capped ±5) — a trigger above only becomes NOT FAIR if |net| stays ≥10 GA one way; smaller nets mean the factors offset each other, so it's still FAIR (hover the badge for what offset what). Lane sum is damped to half weight and duo asymmetry weighted up 1.5x so a team with two coordinated duos can genuinely offset a lead built from several small favorable lanes, instead of summed lanes outweighing everything else by default. v4.9: each lane's SIGNED contribution to the sum is capped at ±15 before it's added — a single blown-out lane can no longer outvote the other four just by itself; the lane's own displayed number, and its heavy/blowout reasons, still show the real uncapped delta. v4.10: per-team autofill weight escalates rather than scaling linearly with count — 1st autofill costs 5, the 2nd (and every one after) costs 15 more each (1→5, 2→20, 3→35) — because 2 autofills means two of five lanes start underwater at once, not just double the risk of one. Shown directly in the TEAM footer ("team GA 60 (+8 duo · 2 autofill)", amber) so it's never just implied by the badge. v4.20: bot-lane duo synergy is deliberately the smallest, most conservative term — capped ±5 on the whole difference (vs ±15 lane sum, ±24 duo bonus) — since "these two champs pair well on paper" is a weaker signal than actual GA/rank/risk.
NOT FAIR is red when stacked against you, amber when in your favor — hover for detail. No more "mixed" — every verdict points a clear direction.
3+ consecutive NOT FAIR-against games flags a possible losing queue — based purely on matchmaking imbalance, never on whether those games were actually won or lost.

Win probability

Logistic on the same net imbalance that decides the verdict above (net = 0.5 × lane sum, capped ±15/lane + team GA gap + 1.5 × duo bonus difference + escalating autofill weight difference + bot synergy difference, capped ±5) — never a separate number that could disagree with the badge. |net| 10 (the NOT FAIR threshold) → ~55/45, |net| 20 → ~60/40. Conservatively calibrated — our analysis database is still small (~34 games), so this ships a deliberately gentle curve rather than an aggressive best-fit; refit planned as the game database grows.
