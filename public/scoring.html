Lane verdicts

v4.21 split: fairness must reflect Riot's matchmaking algorithm; draft quality (counter picks, bot-lane synergy) is the players' own doing in champ select — the two are never mixed together for the verdict anymore (see the Matchmaking/Draft cards below). The lane table itself still shows one COMBINED number per lane (MM + draft) — that's still the practical head-to-head read of "who's actually ahead in this lane" — only the verdict math underneath was split.
adjusted GA (shown per lane) = GA − 5 autofill [MM] − counter (role-weighted) [DRAFT] + 3 duo'd (+9/+12/+15 if the duo includes a jungler, adaptive) [MM] ± 2/division head-to-head rank gap, capped ±8 [MM] ± bot-lane duo synergy, BOTTOM/UTILITY only, capped ±4 [DRAFT]
EVEN ≤5 · favored 6–18 · heavy >18 — except when exactly one laner is autofilled: that lane never reads EVEN (the non-autofilled side gets at least +1) even if the adjusted numbers land inside the band, since off-role risk is an inherent edge the raw gap doesn't fully capture.
[DRAFT] Counter penalty is role-weighted, not flat: −8 top/mid (solo lanes, no one to bail them out), −4 jungle, −3 bot/supp (a partner — jungler ganks, lane duo — can offset a bad matchup). lib/counters.mjs itself stays a clean binary "known counter or not" matrix; the GA cost is scaled at the lane the countered player is actually in.
[MM] The duo bonus applies to whichever lane each half of a duo pair is playing — separate from (and on top of) the team-level duo bonus below. A same-lane duo gets a flat +3. A jungle-inclusive duo's bonus is ADAPTIVE instead — it scales with how far behind the duo'd laner's GA deficit is vs their direct lane opponent (deficit <10 → +9, 10–19 → +12, ≥20 → +15; a duo'd laner who's ahead still gets the baseline +9, never a penalty). Real cases drove this: a duo'd laner going 11/4/12 through a ~34-point GA deficit, and another MVP-ing at 1/8/27 — the box score alone read as a stomp, but the jungler had been propping the lane up the whole game.
[MM] Rank is shown directly on each player (a compact tag next to GA — "E4 · 51%", "M" alone for Master+ with no games yet — hover for full rank + LP + W-L). The lane math also weighs the direct HEAD-TO-HEAD rank gap between the two lane opponents specifically: 2 GA per division of gap (tier×4 + division, Master+ collapsed to tier base), capped at ±8 total, favoring the higher-ranked side. Separate from and additional to the rank-vs-lobby-average signal already baked into each player's GA.
Every champion name in the matchup table (dashed underline, hover) shows its current meta — win/pick/ban rate — from a static per-patch snapshot (lib/champstats.mjs, community-editable and refreshed per patch, same spirit as the counter matrix); info only, no scoring impact. A champion missing from the snapshot just shows as plain text, no hover.
[DRAFT] Bot-lane (ADC+SUPPORT) duo synergy — a duo +2.4%/duo −1.8% chip on the BOTTOM and UTILITY rows, from lib/duosynergy.mjs's per-patch snapshot (op.gg's per-champion "synergies with support" data). The number is the pair's own winrate minus the MEAN of both champs' individual solo winrates (lib/champstats.mjs) — so it isolates "does this specific pairing work," not just "are both champs individually strong." A missing pairing (most ADC+SUPPORT combos are — coverage is each ADC's top-10 partners by play count, not exhaustive) means no chip and no adjustment, never a guessed number. TEAM footer shows both sides' values side by side when both have data.
Smurf-flagged players skip the autofill term entirely (0, not just reduced) — the countered term still applies.

Matchmaking verdict (Riot's algo)

FAIR / NOT FAIR answers one question only: did Riot deal a stacked lobby. Counter picks and bot-lane synergy — what the two teams chose to DO with the lobby they were given — never factor in here; see the Draft verdict card below for those.
  • Team GA gap ≥12 (incl. duo bonus, cap 24)
  • Tier spread inside a team ≥2.5
  • Risk asymmetry (autofill) ≥8
  • 2+ lanes heavily favored one side
  • Blowout lane ≥30 GA
  • Net lane advantage ≥35 GA
Team GA = 65% team avg + 35% avg of top 2 GAs (not a flat average).
Duo bonus per pair: 8 with a winning joint record together, 6 mixed, 5 losing (v4.23, up from 4 — even a losing-joint-record duo coordinates), +0 with no shared prior game to judge by — plus a hot-duo streak bonus on top: +3 if BOTH partners are individually on a 3+ win streak right now, +5 if both are on a 5+ win streak (one partner streaking alone doesn't count). v4.23: when exactly ONE team has any duo at all, that team's summed pair bonus gets a further +3 "presence" bonus — a coordinated pair against a pure-solo team is its own edge (comms, shared calls, synced objective timing) independent of that pair's own record. A team with 2+ duos gets its total (pair bonus + presence, if any) amplified ×1.35 (v4.6, up from ×1.25 — 4 players coordinating vs 2), capped at 24.
netMM = 0.5 × (signed MM-only lane-delta sum, per-lane capped ±15) + team GA gap + 1.5 × duo bonus difference + (enemy autofill weight − your autofill weight) — a trigger above only becomes NOT FAIR if |netMM| stays ≥10 GA one way; smaller nets mean the factors offset each other, so it's still FAIR (hover the badge for what offset what). Lane sum is damped to half weight and duo asymmetry weighted up 1.5x so a team with two coordinated duos can genuinely offset a lead built from several small favorable lanes. Each lane's SIGNED contribution to the sum is capped at ±15 before it's added — a single blown-out lane can no longer outvote the other four just by itself; the lane's own displayed number, and its heavy/blowout reasons, still show the real uncapped MM delta. Autofill weight escalates rather than scaling linearly with count — 1st autofill costs 5, the 2nd (and every one after) costs 15 more each (1→5, 2→20, 3→35). Shown directly in the TEAM footer ("team GA 60 (+8 duo · 2 autofill)", amber) so it's never just implied by the badge.
Three badge states: FAIR green, NOT FAIR red when the lobby was stacked against you, FAVORED amber when it was stacked in your favor (v4.33 — was also labeled "NOT FAIR", color-only; a lobby that leaned your way isn't the same thing as one that leaned against you, so it gets its own word now, not just its own color). Hover any of the three for the actual reason. No more "mixed" — every non-fair verdict points a clear direction.
3+ consecutive NOT FAIR-against games flags a possible losing queue — based purely on matchmaking imbalance, never on whether those games were actually won or lost.

Draft verdict (champ select)

A second, separate verdict — "did the two teams' PICKS create an imbalance," independent of whether matchmaking itself was fair. Shown as a bordered pill under the fairness badge: DRAFT · GOOD +6 / DRAFT · BAD −8 / DRAFT · EVEN, with the fired components (e.g. "Yasuo countered by Annie −8 · bot synergy −3") inline when they fit, else in the tooltip only. Only rendered when at least one draft component actually fired — a game with no counters and no bot-synergy data shows nothing, not a false "EVEN".
netDraft = 0.5 × (signed counter+synergy lane-delta sum, per-lane capped ±15) + (your bot synergy − enemy bot synergy, capped ±5) — BAD at netDraft ≤ −5, GOOD at ≥ +5, EVEN between. Deliberately smaller thresholds than the matchmaking verdict's ±10, since each individual draft input (a counter penalty, a synergy delta) is itself capped much lower than an MM factor like team GA gap.
What each verdict means, hover-for-detail on the pill itself: GOOD your team gained an edge at champion select (counters/synergy) — before the game even started. BAD your team lost champion select — picked into counters or a weak duo; this is on the players, not Riot's matchmaking. EVEN champion select gave neither team a meaningful edge — the picks roughly cancel out.

Win probability

Logistic on netMM + netDraft (the ONE place both sub-nets are summed back together) — winProb predicts the OUTCOME, not fairness, so it has to account for a draft-side stomp (a hard counter, a strong bot duo) the same as a matchmaking-side one, even though the verdict badge (netMM only) doesn't. |netMM+netDraft| 10 → ~55/45, |netMM+netDraft| 20 → ~60/40. Conservatively calibrated — our analysis database is still small (~34 games), so this ships a deliberately gentle curve rather than an aggressive best-fit; refit planned as the game database grows.
