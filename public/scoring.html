How we score

GA: 0–100 pre-game strength from ranked history — nothing from the game itself counts.

Lane verdicts

adjusted GA = GA − 5 autofill − 7 first-time − 8 countered
EVEN ≤5 · favored 6–18 · heavy >18

NOT FAIR triggers (below), but net decides

  • Team GA gap ≥12 (incl. duo bonus, cap 8)
  • Tier spread inside a team ≥2.5
  • Risk asymmetry (autofill/first-time) ≥8
  • 2+ lanes heavily favored one side
  • Blowout lane ≥30 GA
  • Net lane advantage ≥35 GA
  • 2+ first-time champs on your team
Team GA = 65% team avg + 35% avg of top 2 GAs (not a flat average).
A trigger above only becomes NOT FAIR if net (lane deltas + team GA gap) stays ≥10 GA one way — smaller nets mean the factors offset each other, so it's still FAIR (hover the badge for what offset what).
NOT FAIR is red when stacked against you, amber when in your favor — hover for detail. No more "mixed" — every verdict points a clear direction.
